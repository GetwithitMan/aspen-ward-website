Teaching calendar admin

View the calendar · Ward admin

Sign in with your Primary presidency login, or sign in on the ward admin page first.

People and classes

Open a class to see who teaches in it. People who teach together are grouped under one name, with their Usual Sundays. Tick people under Teaches with to group them (3 at most); untick to separate them. Someone in two classes is listed under both. A password is first name plus last initial (kitrickg); Reset puts it back.

Add a teacher

Teachers, subs and Primary presidency. Tick every class they teach in (someone who teaches Nursery one Sunday and CTR another is ticked for both). They show under each class, and each class has its own Teaches with.

Classes: none (presidency)

Notes for a Sunday

A note shows on the public calendar for that Sunday (“Ward conference — lessons are short”). Tick No classes for a Sunday with no teaching, such as stake conference; the day is greyed out and teachers cannot sign up.

Change a Sunday or add a sub

Assign any team to any Sunday, or type in a sub's name. Clear it to make it open. A swap request can also be set here: one teacher needs a sub, or both.
