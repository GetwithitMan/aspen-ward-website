People

A password is the person's first name plus last initial (Kitrick Galloway: kitrickg). Reset puts it back to that.

Add a teacher

Teachers, subs and Primary presidency. If they teach with someone, choose that person; they share that team's Sundays. Presidency members can manage this page.

Notes for a Sunday

A note shows on the public calendar for that Sunday (“Ward conference — lessons are short”). Tick No classes for a Sunday with no teaching, such as stake conference; the day is greyed out and teachers cannot sign up.

Change a Sunday or add a sub

Assign any team to any Sunday, or type in a sub's name. Clear it to make it open.
