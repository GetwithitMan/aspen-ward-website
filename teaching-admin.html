Teaching calendar admin

View the calendar · Ward admin

Sign in with your Primary presidency login, or sign in on the ward admin page first.

People

A password is the person's first name plus last initial (Kitrick Galloway: kitrickg). Reset puts it back to that. Under Teaches with, tick the people who teach together (3 at most); unticking someone separates them. The group's name updates for everyone in it.

Add a teacher

Teachers, subs and Primary presidency. To make two or three people teach together, use Teaches with on their row in the People list above.

Classes and teams

Rename, reorder, add or delete classes, and set each teaching group's usual Sundays (this drives the “Sign up my usual Sundays” button). Who teaches with whom is set in the People list. A class can only be deleted once nobody is in it.

Notes for a Sunday

A note shows on the public calendar for that Sunday (“Ward conference — lessons are short”). Tick No classes for a Sunday with no teaching, such as stake conference; the day is greyed out and teachers cannot sign up.

Change a Sunday or add a sub

Assign any team to any Sunday, or type in a sub's name. Clear it to make it open. A swap request can also be set here: one teacher needs a sub, or both.
